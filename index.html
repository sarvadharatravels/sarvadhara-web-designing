Before you write

Frequently asked questions

Plain answers. If yours is not here, WhatsApp us.

Who owns the domain when the site is live?

You do. Website packages include the first year of domain registration in your (or your firm’s) name. We help you renew from the second year, or you can renew it yourself.

How long does a website take?

Once we have the filled form, your logo and photos, and the agreed advance, we aim to share a preview in 5–7 working days. Go-live follows after you approve the preview. Larger catalogues or bilingual sites may need a little longer, which we will say in the quote.

How many changes can I ask for?

The preview is there so you can correct wording, photos and layout before we publish. Reasonable revisions at that stage are part of the work. New pages or a change of direction after approval are quoted separately, so there are no surprises.

What content do you need from me?

The requirement form lists it: business wording, services, photos, logo, hours, address and the links you want shown. Fill every section and send the logo and photos on WhatsApp or email with the form. If a line is genuinely unknown, say so — we would rather not invent it.

How do payments work?

We start after you approve the quote and pay an advance. The balance is due when you approve the preview, before the site or e-card goes live. We will write the split clearly on the quote. This page never lists prices.

What happens after the first year?

A yearly renewal covers domain, hosting and light maintenance. We confirm the arrangement when we quote, and we remind you before it is due.

Can the site be in Tamil, or in Tamil and English?

Yes. Tick the language on the form. A bilingual Tamil–English website is one of our digital services, and we can write or tidy the wording in both languages if you ask us to.

Where is the site hosted?

On reliable hosting with SSL (a padlock in the browser). We currently publish on GitHub Pages for speed and a secure connection. Your domain points there. You do not need to manage the technical side unless you want to.

What is included in a Digital E-card?

A visiting or business card as a one-tap mobile page — photo, logo, call, WhatsApp, email, map, social buttons, save-contact (vCard) and a QR code. E-invitations cover weddings, birthdays, housewarmings, openings and other events, with photos, a venue map, RSVP on WhatsApp and optional background music. It is a shareable web link, not a printed card. NFC physical cards are a separate service: we design and set up the digital profile, and the card itself is supplied through a print partner.

Can I add digital services later?

Yes. Tick them on the form now, or write to us later. Logo work, Google listing support, QR menus, landing pages and maintenance can be quoted alongside a website or on their own.

Our promise

We will not publish a site until you have seen the preview and said it is ready. We will not invent your words, prices or photographs. We will quote only after we have read your filled form, and we will keep the conversation on WhatsApp or email so you always have a record.
